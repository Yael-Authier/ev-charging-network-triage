\# Project rules



Read this before changing anything in this repository.



\## What this project is



A decision system for a fictional EV charging network operator (Voltora) with

40 stations and a hard constraint of 5 technician visits per week. The question

is which stations to visit, why, and what we expect to recover.



All data is synthetic and generated by `generator/`. Nothing in this repository

describes a real company, and no production reference architecture is documented

here.



\## The four principles



1\. \*\*The final artifact is a decision, not a chart.\*\* The output is a ranked

&#x20;  worklist with reason codes and expected value, not a description of network

&#x20;  state.



2\. \*\*The generator knows the synthetic truth; the analytical layer must not.\*\*

&#x20;  `data/ground\_truth/` may be read only by the evaluation section of

&#x20;  `notebooks/04`. Never from `sql/`, `notebooks/01-03`, `src/policy.py` or

&#x20;  `app/`. A test enforces this.



3\. \*\*Every analytical component must earn its place by changing, validating or

&#x20;  explaining a decision.\*\* Nothing is added to demonstrate a technology.



4\. \*\*The generator is not designed to make our policy win.\*\* The world is built

&#x20;  to be plausible and internally consistent. Which policy performs better is

&#x20;  discovered afterwards. Parameters are never retuned to produce a nicer result.



\## Hard rules



\- \*\*Temporal leakage.\*\* A policy evaluated for week W may only use rows whose

&#x20; `ingested\_at` is at or before the end of week W.

\- \*\*Frozen parameters.\*\* Everything in `generator/config.yml` under `conditions`,

&#x20; `scenarios`, `demand`, `sensitivity` and `falsification` is frozen. Changing

&#x20; any of it requires an entry in `docs/04-decisions-log.md` explaining that it is

&#x20; a bug fix and not a result-driven adjustment.

\- \*\*The falsification thresholds F1-F6 must not be modified.\*\* They were

&#x20; pre-registered before data existed.

\- \*\*No new dependency without a stated reason.\*\* Add it to `requirements.txt`

&#x20; with a one-line comment saying what it is for.

\- \*\*The holdout is evaluated once.\*\* The last 8 weeks of the backtest window.



\## Conventions



\- All code, identifiers, comments, docs and chart labels in \*\*English\*\*.

\- `snake\_case` for columns and functions, `SCREAMING\_SNAKE` for constants.

\- Station ids are `ST-NNN`. Crew ids are `CR-X`. Condition codes are `L1`..`L7`.

\- SQL lives in `sql/`, ordered by layer. Notebooks query; they do not transform.

\- The decision policy lives in `src/policy.py` and is imported by both the app

&#x20; and the evaluation. The evaluation must not redefine it.



\## Freeze order



Metrics -> peer groups -> ERV -> policy -> evaluation.

Do not evaluate before the previous stages are committed.



\## Running things



`python tasks.py check` runs the test suite. See `tasks.py` for other targets.
